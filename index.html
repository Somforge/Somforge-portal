ABOUT US

Welcome to SOMFORGE

SOMFORGE is an educational initiative committed to supporting medical students through accessible, structured and engaging learning experiences.

We believe that becoming a competent doctor requires more than memorising facts. It requires understanding scientific principles, developing clinical reasoning and continuously improving one's knowledge.

Our vision is simple: to contribute to the development of knowledgeable, confident and compassionate future doctors.
