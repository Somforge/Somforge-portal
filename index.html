About SOMFORGE

Supporting the people behind the profession.

SOMFORGE is a medical learning initiative created to support medical students in their academic and professional development.

We bring together structured learning resources, academic guidance and opportunities for collaborative study in one accessible environment.

Our focus is on building a deeper understanding of medicine and developing the knowledge that supports responsible clinical practice.
