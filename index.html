About SOMFORGE

Knowledge is the foundation of good practice.

SOMFORGE is a medical learning initiative built to support students in developing the knowledge, discipline and confidence required throughout medical training. We bring learning resources and academic support into one accessible space, with a simple purpose: make meaningful learning easier to pursue.
