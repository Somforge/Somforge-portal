About SOMFORGE

Supporting the people behind the profession.

SOMFORGE is a medical learning initiative created to support students throughout their academic and professional development.

We bring together learning resources, academic guidance and opportunities for collaborative study in one accessible environment. Our aim is to make it easier for students to find useful material and approach their studies with greater structure.

Because good clinical practice begins with a strong foundation of knowledge, curiosity and consistent learning.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Build strong foundations

Develop a deeper understanding of core medical concepts rather than relying on memorisation alone.

02 / APPLY

Connect learning to practice

Use structured academic resources to support clinical reasoning and the application of knowledge.

03 / PROGRESS

Keep moving forward

Make learning more organised, accessible and consistent throughout medical training.
