Our purpose

Better understanding begins with strong foundations.

Medical education asks students to do more than remember facts. It requires curiosity, clear reasoning and the ability to connect what is learned in the classroom with what is encountered in practice.

SOMFORGE is being built to support that journey: bringing relevant learning resources into a focused environment where students can study with structure and purpose.
