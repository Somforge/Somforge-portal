ABOUT SOMFORGE

Supporting the Journey Through Medical School

Medical training requires a strong understanding of scientific principles, consistent study and the ability to apply knowledge to clinical situations.

SOMFORGE was established to provide medical students with an organised learning environment where they can access teaching materials, review important concepts and prepare for their examinations.

Our focus is on helping students develop a deeper understanding of medicine and build confidence in their academic journey.

Our Purpose

To make quality medical education more accessible through organised learning resources, effective teaching and opportunities for continuous academic development.
